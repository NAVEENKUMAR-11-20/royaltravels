OUR STORY

From a Single Car to a Fleet of Trust

Royal Travels began with one driver, one car, and one mission: to give every passenger the ride they truly deserve. Over the years, our commitment to punctuality, safety, and genuine hospitality has earned us the trust of hundreds of families, executives, and frequent travellers across Tiruchirappalli and beyond.

Today, our premium cabs and luxury vans cover everything from early-morning airport runs to multi-day outstation journeys. We don't just transport — we take care. Every vehicle, every driver, every mile — held to the Royal standard.
